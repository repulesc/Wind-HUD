WIND - USER MANUAL
==================

Wind carries you through a region like the wind: drifting above the
land and the rooftops, skimming the surface of the sea, or gliding
through the water underneath it. No flying needed.


1. START AND STOP
-----------------
Wear the HUD. Then:

    Tap the HUD        rise into the air (or start swimming, if you
                       are standing under water)
    Tap it again       land gently where you are
    Tap twice quickly  stop at once
    Hold the HUD       the menu

The line above the HUD shows where you are and how fast you go,
for example:  GLIDE 4 m · Cruise


2. MOVING
---------
    W / up arrow        forward
    S / down arrow      slow down, then back away slowly
    A, D / arrows       turn
    Shift + A / D       slide sideways
    W twice and hold    boost (faster while you hold it)
    E / Page Up         up
    C / Page Down       down

You speed up and slow down gently, and drift a little when you let go.
In mouselook you steer with the mouse, and under water you swim where
you look.


3. THE THREE LEVELS
-------------------
GLIDE (through the air)
  You float a chosen height above whatever is below you: land, roofs,
  trees or water. Hills and buildings ahead are climbed over by
  themselves. E / C raise and lower the height. The menu has Low (4 m),
  Mid (15 m) and High (40 m).

SURFACE (on the water)
  You skim the water and ride its waves. Over land you hover just above
  the ground, and you settle back on the water when it comes again.

DIVE (under the water)
  You glide through the water between the bottom and the surface. E / C
  rise and sink. The camera stays under the water with you.

Moving between them with E and C:

    Glide    hold C to come down; keep holding at the lowest height to
             settle onto the water (over land: to skim it)
    Surface  tap C to dive (the water must be deep enough)
             tap E to lift back into the air
             over land, hold C to land
    Dive     hold E to rise; at the top, keep holding to surface

You can also jump straight to a level from the menu, or with the
Glide / Surface / Dive buttons if your HUD has them.


4. THE MENU (hold the HUD)
--------------------------
    Glide / Surface / Dive   where you travel
    Low / Mid / High         how high you glide
    Gentle / Cruise / Fast   how fast you go
    Start / Land             switch on or off
    Options                  camera, sound, text above the HUD, help

Wind remembers your height and speed for next time.


5. CHAT COMMANDS
----------------
Type them in local chat (only you are heard):

    /8 glide   /8 surface   /8 dive
    /8 low     /8 mid       /8 high      /8 up      /8 down
    /8 gentle  /8 cruise    /8 fast      /8 speed (the next one)
    /8 land    /8 halt (stop at once)    /8 menu    /8 help
    /8 numbers   live height and speed above the HUD (again: off)

You can put these in gestures to bind them to keys.


6. SETTINGS
-----------
The notecard "Wind Settings" inside the HUD holds speeds, heights, how
floaty it feels, the camera and the sounds, each explained. Edit it
and save it: Wind reads it straight away. To try a value first:

    /8 set speed_cruise 6
    /8 settings          lists every value
    /8 reload            reads the notecard again
    /8 defaults          back to the original values


7. GOOD TO KNOW
---------------
- Wind is not flying: switch Fly off. If you sit down or teleport, Wind
  switches itself off. Tap the HUD to go again.
- Region borders: Wind carries you across. At the edge of the world it
  stops you gently.
- "Something you cannot see is in the way": a ban line or an invisible
  wall. Turn and go another way.
- On land where scripts are not allowed, the HUD buttons stop working,
  but Wind still carries you: hold C to land.
- Wind knows Second Life's own water: the sea, and lakes at sea level.
  Water made of objects (pools, lakes high on a mountain or in the
  sky) it cannot tell apart from air or ground, so Surface and Dive
  only work on the real sea.
- Indoors is not Wind's place: start outside, under the open sky.
- Wind changes your camera while it is on. Options > Camera gives your
  own camera back.
- If your animation overrider fights Wind's poses, switch the AO off
  while you glide.


Enjoy the view.
